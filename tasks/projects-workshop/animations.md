# Projects — motion

- Rocket stage change: 320 ms ease-out fade/scale transition after a mission
  result is acknowledged.
- Final launch: a single restrained lift-off animation after all 15 missions;
  it must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Locked stage press: no bounce or fake interactivity; explain its prerequisite
  on the stage detail screen instead.

The rocket image itself supplies the spectacle. Do not add decorative particles,
continuous glows or competing animations.
