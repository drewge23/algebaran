# Duel — layout

## Mobile (390 × 844)

```text
back · DUEL · rating

              MATCH FOUND
        player       VS       Professorson
        avatar       orbit    portrait
        rating                rating

             5 QUESTIONS · RATING AT STAKE
       rules and a single wide START DUEL button
```

The arena is the hero. The match brief is the only surface; its role is to
explain the stakes and provide the action, not to frame the whole screen.

In play mode, the header contains back, two scores and `round 1 / 5`. The
equation takes the visual centre; three wide answers sit below it.

## Larger viewports

Keep the lobby at a readable maximum width (520 px) and the question area at
520 px. Grow surrounding empty space, not the controls.
